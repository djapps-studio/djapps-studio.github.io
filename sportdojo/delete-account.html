SportDojo licensing account deletion

Applies to SportDojo 1.3.0 and later. Earlier app versions may not offer online licensing deletion; contact support using the procedure below.

SportDojo is published by DJ Apps Studio (Google Play developer name CodeFoundry). Contact support.djapps@gmail.com.

Delete online licensing details

In SportDojo, open Settings, open the access/plans screen, and choose Delete licensing account details. Confirm the request and verify the original licensing Google account. This removes its identifying support-directory entry, including the verified email and attached support/purchase snapshot. A successful response confirms the server processed the request. If the request fails, retry or contact support.

You may also email support.djapps@gmail.com from the licensing Google account with the subject SportDojo licensing deletion. Include your SportDojo Support ID if available. We verify ownership before removing identifying records and process requests requiring manual review within 30 days. Never send a password, payment-card information, Google identity token or purchase token.

Minimal pseudonymous original-demo eligibility, keyed purchase-ownership and deletion markers remain while needed to prevent demo resets, fraud and incorrect ownership. Deletion does not grant another demo. These retained records are not used for advertising.

Delete local training records

Your training profile, readiness processing, schedules and workout history stay on your phone. Use Settings → Delete all app data to remove local app records. Android app-storage controls can also clear them. Online licensing deletion does not remove independently exported files; delete those files separately if desired.

Cancel a paid subscription

Deleting licensing details, clearing app data or uninstalling does not cancel billing. Open Google Play, choose Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, select SportDojo and cancel there. Google Play manages cancellation and applicable refunds.
