SportDojo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Published policy: https://djapps-studio.github.io/sportdojo/privacy.html

SportDOJO is published by DJ Apps Studio. Its Google Play developer name is CodeFoundry. Contact: support.djapps@gmail.com.

In plain language

SportDojo bundles its training content for offline use. Starting or restoring a workout requires a valid signed demo/paid license or publisher evaluation access. The separate 30-day Google-account demo never starts a subscription or automatic charge. A dedicated licensing service receives only the verification/support information described below. Your profile choices, schedules, session history and readiness answers are processed and stored on your device.

Information kept on your device

SportDojo may store:

  • your display name, selected gender (including a prefer-not-to-say choice), and optional height in centimetres and weight in kilograms;
  • your male/female trainer preference, which can be changed independently of gender;
  • selected sports and your active sport;
  • optional Cricket or Baseball role;
  • experience level, primary goal, preferred session duration, training days, and rest days;
  • resistance-band availability, theme, and reduced-motion preferences;
  • favorites, recently previewed movements, and optional interval sound/haptic preferences;
  • editable seven-day schedules;
  • routine history, completion state, skipped exercises, and prescription snapshots;
  • non-diagnostic “too easy,” “suitable,” or “too difficult” feedback;
  • general fatigue or soreness feedback used conservatively for later workload selection; and
  • an active-session checkpoint, including current exercise, set/repetition or timer state, pause state, and whether completion feedback is pending.

This information is used only on the device to provide app features such as deterministic routines, schedules, session restoration, and history.

Your display name personalises the interface. Gender supplies a default trainer illustration when you choose to match your profile; it does not constrain later trainer selection. Height and weight are optional profile information and are not used for medical assessment, calorie estimates, or exercise-intensity prescriptions. You can edit or remove optional details in your profile.

Readiness check

Readiness answers are evaluated temporarily to decide whether a session should proceed, be reduced, or be blocked. SportDojo does not store the symptom details you select and does not ask for a diagnosis or narrative medical history. At most, it may retain that a session was skipped and the date.

Readiness information is not used to diagnose a condition or provide an injury-adapted routine.

Information not requested

SportDojo does not ask you to enter an email, password, exact date of birth, diagnosis, injury history, medication information, or payment-card details. Optional online licensing uses Google sign-in as explained below. Your display name does not have to be your legal name. It is designed not to request notification access or access location, microphone, camera, contacts, activity recognition, body sensors, calendar, SMS, or broad device storage.

Collection, sharing, sale, advertising, and analytics

SportDojo uses a dedicated licensing backend for account-bound access. It has no analytics, advertising, or remote-configuration service. App-entered training information is not sent to this backend, automatically shared with other apps, or sold. An explicit history export is written only to the file you select through Android. It does not use information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Exercise demonstrations play videos bundled with the app through the native Android player. The bundled model renderer uses Android WebView where available. These components do not visit websites or load a remote video service, and your name, measurements and session history are not sent to the renderer.

This statement depends on the released app matching the reviewed source and dependency set. The publisher reviews the final merged manifest, included SDKs, and release artifact before publication. If a future version adds any off-device transfer, this policy and the relevant in-app disclosure and Play Data Safety answers must be updated before that feature is released.

App-store and operating-system services

Google Play subscriptions

Google Play Billing handles monthly/yearly subscriptions, payment processing and localized prices under Google's terms. Paid plans charge only when you explicitly subscribe and renew until cancelled in Google Play. The separate 30-day demo requires no subscription or payment method and never starts automatic billing. Prices are displayed from Google Play before purchase. SportDojo never receives payment-card details.

Google-account licensing and the 30-day demo

Choosing Start/Recover demo, Restore purchases, purchase verification or verified licensing deletion can use Google Credential Manager, Play Integrity and SportDojo's dedicated HTTPS licensing service on Google Cloud/Firebase/Firestore. Ordinary app launch and foregrounding read only the saved local license for licensing; update checks are separate. The Google account used for licensing may differ from the purchasing Play account.

Google provides an identity token and verified email. The server validates it, the installation-key proof and (for access issuance) Play Integrity's app recognition, Play licensing and device integrity verdicts. A secret-derived pseudonymous account identifier and original demo start date enforce one original 30-day demo per Google account. Reinstallation, local deletion and account-detail deletion do not reset eligibility. The developer does not receive your Google password.

The support directory stores your verified Google email, Support ID, creation/verification dates, original demo dates and operation type. Successful paid verification adds a dated snapshot of the allowed product/base plan, paid expiry, renewal status, test-purchase flag and order reference when supplied by Google. It is a snapshot, not a continuously refreshed payment status. The authenticated, read-only owner dashboard accesses only this licensing metadata. It cannot see your profile, readiness answers, measurements, schedules or training history, and cannot grant, cancel or refund access.

Purchase tokens are sent by HTTPS to verify the actual entitlement and acknowledge completed purchases with Google Play. Raw purchase tokens, Google identity tokens and Integrity tokens are not retained in Firestore or application logs. The server retains keyed purchase-owner hashes to prevent a purchase being claimed by another account. Bounded admission counters and one-use request hashes expire through a three-day Firestore TTL policy; platform deletion can occur later. Google Cloud operational logs are configured for seven-day retention; mandatory platform audit retention is separate.

A signed device-bound demo license ends at the original 30-day expiry. Paid offline certificates last at most seven days and never past the verified entitlement expiry. License records and monotonic clock anchors are encrypted in app-private no-backup storage. A phone reboot requires explicit online verification; changing the wall clock cannot extend access. Publisher-provided evaluation access is separate from purchases, excluded from history exports and backup. Saved history, export, deletion and safety remain available without access.

Google Play, Android and your device manufacturer may independently process installation, security, purchase, device or diagnostic information under their policies. SportDojo does not attach training data to these requests. Platform-provided aggregate crash or ANR information may be available to the publisher. Review your app-store and device privacy controls.

Google Play app updates

SportDojo automatically checks update availability through Google Play when opened and offers a manual check in Settings. Google Play processes device metadata, the application version and installed module or asset-pack information to determine availability and expected download size. SportDojo does not attach profile information, readiness answers, schedules or training history to these requests. Google Play performs downloads and installation under Google's terms; its automatic-update preference is controlled in the Play Store. See Google's in-app update data guidance. This service is separate from local training storage and Google Play Billing.

Device storage, security, and backup

Local information is protected by Android's application sandbox and the security of your device. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so keep your device and operating system protected.

SportDojo disables or restricts automatic cloud backup for its local preferences, readiness state, schedules, history, and checkpoints so that this information is not uploaded unexpectedly through Android backup. Device manufacturers and operating-system tools outside SportDojo may have their own behaviour; review your device settings as well.

History transfer is optional and uses Android's file picker. Exported files contain session history, feedback and exercise snapshots, not readiness symptom answers or profile measurements. You choose the file location. If you select a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own policy. SportDojo does not automatically upload it or receive a copy. Import adds validated history without restoring an active workout or replacing your profile. Exported files are outside the app's private storage; protect them and delete them separately. Clearing or uninstalling SportDojo does not delete files you exported.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to your operating system's behaviour.

Use Settings → Delete all app data to clear SportDojo preferences, schedules, session history, and active-session state from the app. Android's system settings can also clear the app's storage. Online licensing deletion is separate: use the access screen's Delete licensing account details action and verify the Google account, or follow the licensing deletion page. The verified email and identifying support-directory entry are removed after ownership verification. Minimal original-demo eligibility, keyed purchase-ownership and deletion markers remain while needed to prevent demo resets, fraud and incorrect ownership; they are not used for advertising. Deletion does not cancel Google Play subscriptions or erase exported files. Contact support for help; requests needing manual ownership review are processed within 30 days.

Adults only

SportDojo is intended for adults aged 18 or older. It is not directed to children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health information

SportDojo provides general fitness education. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. See the in-app Safety Information before exercising.

Changes to this policy

If SportDojo's data handling changes, the publisher will update this policy and its effective date before or with the applicable app release. Material new data use requires an appropriate in-app disclosure and consent where required.

Questions

For privacy questions, email support.djapps@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can respond. Do not include sensitive health information. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations; you may request its deletion.

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement. Opening the public policy uses your external browser; it does not upload your local SportDOJO profile or history. The app's Settings screen links to the published policy.
