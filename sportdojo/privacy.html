Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains how SportDOJO, an Android app from DJ Apps Studio published on Google Play under the developer name CodeFoundry, handles information. For privacy enquiries, contact support.djapps@gmail.com.

1. In plain language

SportDOJO works offline and does not require an account. Information entered in the app is processed and stored on your device. SportDOJO has no app backend, advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization or remote-configuration service. It does not automatically transmit your profile, exercise history, schedules or readiness answers to us or to other parties.

2. Information on your device

Depending on the features you use, SportDOJO may store:

  • Your display name, selected gender (including a prefer-not-to-say choice), and optional height and weight.
  • Your selected sports, active sport, optional Cricket or Baseball role, experience level, goals and preferred session duration.
  • Training and rest days, seven-day schedules and resistance-band availability.
  • Trainer, appearance and reduced-motion preferences.
  • Exercise prescriptions, session history, completion and skipped-exercise records, and general difficulty, fatigue or soreness feedback.
  • An active-session checkpoint, including the current exercise, set, repetitions or timer, pause state and pending completion feedback.

This information is used locally to provide routines, schedules, history and session restoration. Your display name need not be your legal name. Gender may supply a default trainer choice and does not limit later trainer selection. Optional height and weight are profile information, not medical assessments, calorie estimates or intensity prescriptions.

3. Readiness checks

Readiness answers are evaluated temporarily to decide whether a session should proceed, be reduced or be blocked. SportDOJO does not store selected symptom details, diagnoses or narrative medical histories. At most, it may record that a session was skipped and the date. Readiness checks do not diagnose conditions or provide injury-adapted exercise.

4. Permissions and demonstrations

SportDOJO does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, calendar, body sensors or activity-recognition data. It does not ask for payment information, account credentials or unrestricted device storage access.

Exercise videos, models, pictures and scripts are bundled with the app. Demonstrations use local Android video playback and an Android system WebView for the bundled 3D guide. The app does not load an online video service or send your profile and history to the renderer.

5. Collection, sharing and platform services

SportDOJO does not transmit app-entered data off your device, sell it, use it for advertising or share it with other apps. There is no publisher-operated app account or server database.

Google Play, Android and your device manufacturer may independently process installation, security, device, crash or diagnostic information under their own terms and privacy policies. The publisher may receive platform-provided aggregate quality reports, such as crash or ANR reports. These platform services do not create a SportDOJO backend or give us your local profile or exercise database. Review your app-store and device privacy controls for their separate practices.

6. Storage, backup and security

Local information is protected by Android's application sandbox and your device's security controls. SportDOJO disables automatic backup and excludes its local preferences, schedules, history and checkpoints from Android backup and device-transfer rules. Device manufacturers or tools outside the app may have their own behaviour.

No device or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and operating system protected.

7. Retention and deletion

Local app information generally remains until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to Android's behaviour. Use Settings → Delete all app data to clear SportDOJO preferences, schedules, session history and active-session state. Android's app settings can also clear app storage.

There is no SportDOJO account or separate server-side account to delete. We cannot remotely inspect or delete information stored only on your device.

8. Support correspondence and this website

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message and attachments. We use it to respond and resolve your request, retain it as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations, and may use our email service provider to handle it. You can request deletion of correspondence we hold, subject to those obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive health information unless necessary for your request.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and technical information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. These pages do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms. Website hosting and email support are separate from the offline app.

9. Adults and health information

SportDOJO is intended for generally healthy adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children. It provides general fitness education, not injury treatment or rehabilitation.

This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

10. Your choices and policy updates

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or otherwise control personal information. For information stored only on your device, use the app and Android controls described above. For correspondence sent to DJ Apps Studio, contact support.djapps@gmail.com.

We will update this policy and its effective date if SportDOJO's features or data handling change. Material new data use will receive an appropriate in-app disclosure and consent where required. For questions about this policy, email the contact above.
