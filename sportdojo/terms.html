3. Safe use

Read and follow the in-app Safety Information, exercise instructions, technique cues, stop cues, equipment guidance, and displayed limits.

Do not begin a session if the readiness check blocks it. Stop exercising for sharp or increasing pain, chest symptoms, severe or unusual breathlessness, dizziness or faintness, numbness, sudden swelling, loss of coordination, or inability to maintain safe technique. Seek urgent or emergency help for potentially serious symptoms and qualified healthcare advice for medical concerns.

Use a clear area and stable, non-slip surface. Move in a comfortable range and do not force movement or exercise through pain. Inspect resistance bands before use and control their tension and release. You are responsible for your surroundings, equipment condition, and adherence to instructions.
