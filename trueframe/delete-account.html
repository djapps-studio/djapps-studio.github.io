Delete licensing account details

Android release scope: The Google-account demo and subscription terms below apply to TrueFrame Android 0.8.0 and later licensing-enabled releases. That update is being prepared and is not yet available on Google Play. Earlier offline releases remain covered by the previous privacy policy. This update does not change the separate Windows app.

In Android, open Demo & subscription (or Settings > Demo & subscription), choose Delete licensing account details and verify ownership with Google. This remains available without active demo/paid access. No working Play Integrity verdict or purchase is required for deletion verification.

If you cannot use the app, email support.djapps@gmail.com with subject "Delete TrueFrame licensing account details". We will guide you through ownership verification; an email or Support ID alone is not proof. Never send passwords, one-time login codes, purchase tokens or payment details. Requests are processed within 30 days, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Verified deletion removes identifying licensing directory details (verified email, Support ID and dated support/purchase snapshot). Original keyed account/demo eligibility dates, deletion markers and hashed purchase ownership/linked-token records remain to prevent repeat demos and purchase transfer for the life of the licensing service, subject to applicable requirements. Fresh disclosed verification can recreate support details without restarting the original demo.

This does not cancel Google Play subscriptions, erase Google's records or delete original media, exports or app-private files. Cancel separately in Google Play; use Android Clear storage/uninstall and authorized media controls for local information. Shared/backed-up copies require separate management.
