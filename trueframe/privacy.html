Android release scope: The Google-account demo and subscription terms below apply to TrueFrame Android 0.8.0 and later licensing-enabled releases. That update is being prepared and is not yet available on Google Play. Earlier offline releases remain covered by the previous privacy policy. This update does not change the separate Windows app.

TrueFrame Privacy Policy
DJ Apps Studio
Effective date: 21 October 2026 - Android licensing update

1. Local media processing
TrueFrame is a gallery, organizer, editor, audio/video player and frame inspection app. Photos, videos, audio, filenames, folders, embedded location, recognized text, labels, editing recipes and media metadata are processed on your device. TrueFrame does not upload them for licensing or AI processing. AI, text-recognition and image-labeling models are bundled. There are no ads, app analytics service or remote AI/model downloads. Hidden albums are not encrypted vaults.

2. Permissions and local records
You control media permissions, selected files and authorized folders through Android. Optional media-location permission reads location already embedded in files; it does not track live location. TrueFrame does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs or unrestricted all-files access. Favorites, albums, drafts, playback positions and settings are stored locally. Android controls authorization for modifying, trashing, restoring and permanently deleting media. File pickers request local-only documents. Revoking access limits affected features.

3. Google-account demo and licensing
You can explicitly sign in with Google to activate or recover one independent 30-day demo for that Google account in TrueFrame. No payment details or subscription are needed. The original eligibility dates persist across reinstalls and account-detail deletion; the demo never automatically converts to a paid plan. Ordinary launch, resume and media use read local licensing state without automatic sign-in, Play Integrity or licensing-service requests.

When you choose activation, recovery, paid purchase verification or deletion, Google processes sign-in information. TrueFrame sends a short-lived Google identity token, an installation public key, a nonce and signed request to the dedicated TrueFrame licensing service over HTTPS. Activation and paid verification also use Google Play Integrity, which processes app, licensing, device and security signals. Purchase verification sends the purchase token to our service for validation and acknowledgement with Google Play. Raw identity and purchase tokens are processed for these requests and are not retained in the licensing database or app backups.

The service keeps app-specific keyed account identifiers and original demo start/expiry, hashed purchase ownership/linked-token records, and short-lived request/quota/replay counters. A separately enabled licensing support directory retains only verified sign-in email, TrueFrame Support ID, creation/last-verification times, original demo dates, operation and a dated Google-verified purchase snapshot: product/plan, subscription state, expiry, renewal/test status and eligible order reference. Collection of identifying directory details requires this disclosed client request and server enablement. No historical email is guessed or backfilled. Other DJ Apps Studio apps use separate customer databases and keys; a restricted owner dashboard reads licensing metadata only, without joining customer identities across apps. A Support ID is a reference, not ownership proof.

These data support app functionality, account recovery, purchases, fraud prevention and licensing support. The verified Google sign-in account may differ from the Play purchasing account. Google Cloud/Firebase provide hosting/database and infrastructure processing. Google controls its sign-in, Integrity and Play services under its policies. Infrastructure request/security logs may include IP/network metadata; application code does not log request bodies, tokens or media. Google SDKs may process per-installation identifiers, app/device information, feature-use and performance/error metrics for service operation, security and diagnostics/usage analysis. ML Kit recognition remains on-device; its documented diagnostic signals do not include input media. Recognition background upload components remain removed, but enabling networking means the earlier blanket network-free disclosure no longer applies. Licensing service resources are hosted in Mumbai; Google may process service/support data elsewhere under its terms. We do not sell data or use licensing information for advertising.

4. Paid plans and offline access
Monthly and annual plans use Google Play Billing, which handles payment and its required service data; we do not receive payment-card details. Prices and terms appear before purchase. Paid access is granted only after server verification. Installation-bound signed certificates and an authenticated local clock anchor permit offline use until certificate expiry. Paid certificates last at most seven days and never beyond verified purchase expiry; cancellation/refund/revocation detection offline can therefore lag. Restarting the phone or losing trusted time requires explicit online verification. Changing device time, restoring settings or deleting app storage does not create a new demo.

5. Sharing and exports
Exports are local new files. TrueFrame does not automatically upload them. Share/Open with hands only selected files to the app you choose, whose behavior follows its own policies. Copying text or a Support ID uses Android's clipboard. Original files may include personal metadata; photo export offers Remove location metadata, enabled by default. Separate photo-backup services may back up shared media under their own settings. Settings profiles contain preferences, never licensing certificates, identity/purchase tokens, account eligibility or signing keys. Android automatic app backup is disabled.

6. Retention and deletion
Local app records normally remain until removed through app controls, Clear storage or uninstall. Original media, shared-storage exports and shared copies remain separately managed; temporary files may be cleared by TrueFrame or Android. Deleting a source file does not necessarily erase every local organization record.

Use Settings > Demo & subscription > Delete licensing account details to verify ownership through Google and request deletion. Identifying directory details are removed after verified deletion; remaining nonessential information and support requests are handled within 30 days, subject to legal obligations. Fresh disclosed verification can recreate the support directory. Original demo eligibility, keyed account/deletion markers and purchase-ownership/retirement hashes remain to prevent repeated demos and purchase transfer for the life of the licensing service, subject to applicable requirements. This does not cancel a subscription, delete local media or erase Google's records. Cancel separately in Google Play. You can also request deletion at https://djapps-studio.github.io/trueframe/delete-account.html or contact support.djapps@gmail.com; ownership verification is required, and a Support ID/email alone is not proof.

7. Security and support
Connections use HTTPS. Licensing certificates are server signed and installation bound; local licensing cache/keys use Android Keystore and non-backup storage. Firestore mobile/client reads and writes are denied; the restricted owner dashboard uses authenticated, authorized server reads. No security system is absolute. Keep your device and Google account secure. Email support receives the correspondence and attachments you choose to send; our email provider processes it, and we retain it as needed for support and legal obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details, purchase tokens or personal media unnecessarily.

8. Children and changes
TrueFrame is a general media utility, not a child-directed service. Google-account and payment services have their own eligibility requirements. Contact support regarding a child's personal information. Material licensing/privacy changes require renewed local agreement before activation or purchase. This version supersedes the earlier network-free Android policy; the separate Windows app is not changed by this Android release.
