Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TrueFrame Android application, provided by DJ Apps Studio, accesses, uses, stores, and shares information. It also explains the separate data practices of the online services available within the app and of this policy website.

1. Overview

TrueFrame is a photo and video gallery, organizer, editor, and local video inspection utility. Its gallery functions process authorized media on your device and can work offline. TrueFrame does not require a TrueFrame account and does not provide a studio-operated media upload, cloud backup, or cloud synchronization service.

A separate, optional YouTube section connects to Google services for online search and playback. On-device text recognition and image labeling use Google ML Kit, whose diagnostic data practices are described below. Local media processing does not mean that these providers never receive technical or usage information.

2. Information Accessed and Stored on Your Device

Depending on the features you use and the access you grant, TrueFrame handles:

  • Photos, videos, and audio already contained in videos, for viewing, playback, editing, comparison, collage creation, frame inspection, and export.
  • Media information such as filenames, folders, dates, file sizes, formats, dimensions, duration, and available camera metadata, for organization, search, sorting, details, and duplicate review.
  • Location already embedded in media, when accessible and authorized, for photo details and location-based grouping. TrueFrame does not track your live device location.
  • Recognized text and generic image labels, for local search and user-requested copying. Recognition does not identify named people.
  • Favorites, album names and membership, hidden-album flags, editing recipes, saved media positions, cover choices, and appearance and playback preferences, to remember your choices.
  • Locally saved YouTube timestamp bookmarks and playback preferences. The app keeps up to the most recent 100 bookmarks.

TrueFrame does not automatically send your gallery photos, videos, recognized text, or album records to DJ Apps Studio or YouTube. Recognition-provider diagnostic information is discussed in section 5.

3. Permissions and Your Choices

Photo and video access supports ongoing browsing and management of your authorized library. On supported Android versions, you can grant access to selected media instead of the full library. You can also import individual files or authorize storage folders using Android's selection controls, including supported removable storage. If broad access is denied, features remain limited to files the app can access.

Optional media-location access lets TrueFrame read available location metadata already stored in media. The app does not request live GPS tracking, camera capture, microphone recording, contacts, SMS, or call-log access. Playing or editing existing video audio does not record your microphone.

Internet and network-status access support the online YouTube section. Recognition services may also transmit technical diagnostic information. Media playback may keep the device awake while needed. TrueFrame does not request unrestricted all-files access.

Android authorization is used where required for modifying, trashing, restoring, or permanently deleting media. You can change app permissions in Android settings. Revoking access limits features that depend on the affected files or folders.

4. Exports Sharing and Metadata

Edits and other outputs can be saved as new files in device media storage. When you choose Share, Open with, or a related action, selected media is provided to the app or destination you choose. That destination's privacy practices apply to its use of the information.

Original files may contain location, camera, date, or other metadata. Sharing an original does not automatically strip that metadata. Photo export includes a Remove location metadata option, enabled by default; you can choose to retain available location metadata for supported outputs. Other metadata may be preserved depending on the format and operation.

When you choose to copy recognized text or a timestamp link, that content is placed on Android's clipboard. If you paste it elsewhere, the receiving app or service can process it. Hidden albums hide references from normal gallery browsing; they are not an encrypted vault or protection from other apps that have access to the files.

5. On Device Recognition and Provider Diagnostics

TrueFrame uses bundled Google ML Kit text recognition and generic image labeling to analyze images on the device. These features do not require uploading your gallery images for recognition or downloading recognition models at runtime.

Google documents that ML Kit may collect technical information for diagnostics and usage analytics, including device and app information, installation identifiers, performance measurements, feature configuration, event information, and error codes. Collection can depend on the provider's applicable version, configuration, and service behavior. This technical information is separate from your gallery image contents and local search records.

Google's ML Kit data disclosure guidance describes HTTPS protection for the diagnostic information it collects and states that this information is not transferred onward to third parties. See also the ML Kit terms and privacy information and Google Privacy Policy. TrueFrame does not include a separate DJ Apps Studio analytics or advertising service.

6. YouTube Google Cookies and Advertisements

If you use the online section, TrueFrame loads YouTube browsing pages and an official embedded video player. Your connection to YouTube sends information to Google, including connection and playback information. Google's services may process IP addresses, browser or device information, cookies and other identifiers, searches, viewing activity, and interactions with content or ads, depending on service behavior and your settings.

The online section permits cookies, including third-party cookies, and website storage. YouTube may display advertisements and use information for service delivery, security, measurement, personalization, and advertising under its policies. The native gallery does not display studio-inserted ads, but the app as a whole should not be considered ad-free.

Your gallery media is not supplied to YouTube. YouTube bookmarks and playback preferences saved by TrueFrame remain on the device; opening a bookmarked video contacts YouTube. Activity may be associated with a Google account depending on the provider's sign-in state and account settings. TrueFrame does not operate a Google account or control Google's retention practices.

The Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service apply to those services. You can review Google-held activity through Google My Activity and your Google account privacy controls. Closing the YouTube screen or clearing saved timestamps does not itself delete provider cookies, account activity, or data held by Google. External links opened in another app are governed by that app's and website's policies.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local app records, including organization choices, recognized text, image labels, editing recipes, preferences, and bookmarks, generally remain until removed through available app controls, Android's Clear storage action, or uninstalling the app. Deleting a source media file does not necessarily erase every related local search or organization record. Saved YouTube bookmarks can be removed using Clear saved.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-managed local information under normal Android behavior. It does not automatically delete original media, exported files in shared storage, copies shared with another app, or information held by Google. You can manage original and exported files through authorized media actions or other device tools. Android manages the retention period for trashed media.

Automatic Android backup is disabled for TrueFrame's app data. A separate device or photo-backup service may still back up media in shared storage according to its own settings. TrueFrame has no account to delete. For deletion of Google-held information, use Google's controls or contact Google under its policies.

If you contact us for support or a privacy request, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it to respond and resolve your request and retain it as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of correspondence we hold, subject to those obligations. Avoid sending personal gallery files unless needed for your request.

8. Security and Disclosures

TrueFrame uses Android's app storage and permission controls to limit local access. Its online content uses HTTPS connections, and Google's recognition diagnostic guidance describes encryption in transit. Hidden albums do not add encryption. No device, storage system, or internet service can guarantee absolute security.

DJ Apps Studio does not sell your gallery media and does not operate a service that automatically collects it. User-directed sharing and the provider processing described above can transfer information outside your device. Information you send directly to us for support may be handled by our email service provider or disclosed when required by applicable law.

9. Children and Online Content

TrueFrame is a general media utility and does not provide a dedicated child mode or parental supervision service. YouTube content, account eligibility, and restrictions are governed by YouTube and applicable law. Local media permissions do not establish an age restriction on online content. Parents and guardians should manage device and provider account access where appropriate. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information directly to DJ Apps Studio.

10. Privacy Questions and Changes

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise control personal information. For information stored only on your device, use the controls described above. For information you have sent directly to DJ Apps Studio, contact support.djapps@gmail.com. For Google-held information, use Google's privacy controls and request procedures.

We may update this policy when TrueFrame's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date and last updated date above identify the current policy. Review this page for updates.
