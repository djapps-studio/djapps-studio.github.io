Please read this short agreement before using FretVela.

Music guidance is an estimate. Chord, key, tempo, beat, likely-next-chord, capo, and Suggested Strumming Pattern results can be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. Results vary with recording quality, background noise, vocals and other instruments, microphone and device characteristics, tuning, arrangement, and overlapping sounds. Check musical guidance yourself whenever accuracy matters.

Live Practice analyzes nearby sound locally only while its screen is active. Live Practice and Tuner microphone audio is processed in memory and discarded, and their capture stops when the app is no longer visible. Learn & Analyze Song records only after you start it, pauses capture when the app is no longer visible, and keeps the completed recording privately on this device so you can play it back, review it, and reanalyze it later. A completed analysis is staged as a private local draft before Song Ready so it can be recovered after an app restart; it does not appear in Saved Songs until you choose Save. Discarding a new unsaved song map or deleting a saved song also deletes its retained recording. Imported practice audio remains on your device. Practice history stores only local session metadata such as date, mode, duration, confidence-qualified tempo/key/capo values, and chord names; weak values are omitted, and history does not store Live or Tuner microphone audio, imported-audio content, paths, or links. FretVela has no account, cloud backup, analytics, or audio upload feature. Developer builds may log bounded chord and prediction timing metadata locally for testing, never raw audio; release builds do not emit those timing records.

Learning Path saves your self-reported milestone completions, checklists, and last viewed lesson privately on this device. It does not grade your playing or guarantee learning outcomes.

Corrections you make to a song map, estimated key, tempo, or Recommended Capo are stored locally with that saved song. The original on-device estimates remain available for comparison and reanalysis. FretVela does not download, rip, extract, or intercept audio from YouTube, Spotify, or other streaming services; Live Practice only analyzes sound that reaches the phone microphone, and Local Audio analyzes only a file you explicitly choose.

Backing tracks and practice tools are aids, not a promise of musical accuracy, progress, compatibility, or uninterrupted availability. You are responsible for the music and other content you play, import, share, or analyze, including copyright, licences, permissions, and third-party platform terms.

Pending or cancelled purchase dialogs do not grant access.

After subscription access ends, saved results and history remain viewable and deletable; new premium playback, practice, analysis and content editing require access.
