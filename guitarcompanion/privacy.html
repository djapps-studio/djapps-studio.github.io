Privacy Policy

Updated: 20 October 2026

AuraFret (formerly Guitar Companion) is a local-first Android application published by DJ Apps Studio. Google sign-in and a small Google Cloud licensing backend support the 30-day demo and paid access. It has no analytics, advertising, music cloud synchronization, or audio-upload feature. The app does not sell personal information. Google Play processes subscription transactions as described below.

Microphone and audio

The app requests microphone access only after the user chooses Start Listening, Start Recording, or Start Tuning.

  • Live Practice and Guitar Tuner process microphone samples in memory while their screens are visible, then discard those samples. Their capture stops when the app is no longer visible.
  • Learn & Analyze Song records only after explicit user action. Capture pauses when the app is no longer visible. A completed reference recording is retained in app-private storage so the user can review, play, correct, and reanalyze the learned song.
  • Local Audio Practice analyzes only a file the user explicitly chooses through Android's system file picker. The app does not copy that audio into cloud storage or upload it.
  • AuraFret does not download, rip, extract, bypass protection for, or intercept audio from YouTube, Spotify, or other streaming services.

Local data

Learned-song profiles, unsaved recovery drafts, song-map corrections, appearance and practice preferences (including up to 50 saved-song resume positions, speeds and difficulty choices), agreement acceptance, self-reported Learning Path milestone/checklist progress and last viewed lesson, and practice-history metadata are stored locally on the device. A practice-history entry may contain a name, date, mode, duration, chord names, and confidence-qualified key, tempo, or capo estimates. History does not retain Live/Tuner microphone audio, imported-audio content, file paths, or URIs.

Developer builds may write bounded chord and prediction timing metadata to the local Android diagnostic log for testing. These logs contain no raw audio, user names, or audio-file paths and are not uploaded; release builds do not emit these timing records.

Analysis probabilities are transient unless a bounded debug diagnostic is explicitly part of a local debug profile. A saved learned song may retain one possible alternative chord identity for an untouched medium-confidence segment; it does not retain the source frame probabilities for that alternative.

Android Auto Backup is disabled for the application, so this app data is not backed up through Android's automatic cloud-backup mechanism.

Licensing support directory

After you accept the updated agreement and explicitly verify access, the publisher can retain your verified Google sign-in email, app-specific Support ID, directory creation/last-verification time, original demo dates and a dated limited purchase snapshot (product, plan, subscription state, paid-through date, renewal setting, test flag and validated Play order reference) for licensing support. The sign-in account may differ from the Play purchasing account. The owner console is read-only and does not query Play or change access when opened. Account/demo verification preserves the last purchase snapshot; it can become stale after a refund or renewal. No audio, songs, names or device identifiers are added to this directory. Identifying directory records are retained until verified deletion or the end of this licensing service. Verified deletion removes the identifying directory/snapshot while preserving the original trial eligibility and purchase-ownership hashes for the service lifetime to prevent resets or reassignment. A minimal keyed deletion timestamp also remains to prevent earlier in-flight verification recreating the directory. A Support ID is a reference, not proof of ownership. Older app requests do not create identifying records; no email is guessed or backfilled. App updates and fresh verification are needed before details appear.

Optional personal practice

The four-slide tour and Home allow optional experience, guitar type, practice goal, a 5/10/15-minute budget and gentle haptic feedback (off by default). These choices, up to 12 named practice plans, up to 500 library favourites and manually logged practice minutes are stored in a separate local preference file. The activity log retains at most 366 active-day records, with daily totals capped at 180 minutes. The seven-day chart, streak and encouragement are calculated from these explicit logs; neither opening the app nor listening to a demonstration counts as practice or a playing assessment.

Onboarding and lesson technique examples are original, bounded eight-second synthesized samples. They use no microphone, require an explicit Play action and stop when their owner screen closes or the app is no longer visible. Their generated audio files are temporary local cache files, deleted on normal stop/release; interruption can leave cache until Android clears it. This audio is separate from music detection and full subscription practice tools.

None of this personal practice data is uploaded, included in a song ZIP backup or restored by Restore purchases. Home lets you edit choices; Library lets you remove favourites and saved plans. Settings offers a confirmed Clear personal practice data action, preserving songs, Learning Path progress, purchases and agreement acceptance.

Optional Google Play app updates

AuraFret can check for app updates while Home or Settings is visible and idle. Google Play processes device metadata, the installed application version, and installed module/asset-pack information to determine availability and download size under its own terms. The app does not send microphone audio, songs, lesson progress, history or recordings to this service. Google Play requires connectivity; a failed update check does not prevent local tools from working.

Update prompts are optional. Google Play asks for confirmation before a flexible update downloads. The app asks you to restart only through an explicit Restart to update action on an idle screen; it does not force updates during recording or playback. Choosing Later stores only the offered version code and a local timestamp to hide that version's reminder for 24 hours. These bounded preferences are separate from songs, purchases and agreement acceptance, are excluded from song ZIP exports, and are removed with app data. Settings allows a manual check despite a deferred reminder. The app creates no push notifications or update server.

User-controlled song backup

Settings can export saved song maps, edits and retained recordings to an unencrypted ZIP at a location you select through Android's document picker. The selected document provider may upload or synchronize that file under its own policy. AuraFret itself does not upload it. Export is optional and available without subscription access. Protect that ZIP and delete external copies separately; deleting a song in the app does not delete an exported copy.

Restore adds absent song IDs and preserves existing songs. Backups exclude unfinished drafts, imported-file permissions, history, learning progress, preferences, agreement acceptance and subscriptions. Restore purchases remains a separate Google Play access check. Working restore files are temporary and removed when the operation completes; process interruption can leave temporary cache files until Android clears the cache.

Permissions and network access

The packaged app declares RECORD_AUDIO, INTERNET and com.android.vending.BILLING, together with signature-protected service permissions contributed by the Google credential SDKs. It does not request broad storage, location, network-state, wake-lock, notification, or microphone foreground-service access. HTTPS licensing traffic is sent only when verification is needed and explicitly started; ordinary tools use valid local certificates. All core analysis runs on the device. Google Play handles network communication for billing and optional app updates through its service.

Google Play subscriptions

Purchases use Google Play, which processes payment information under its terms and privacy policy. AuraFret does not receive card details. Explicit Google sign-in activates/retrieves a 30-day demo without purchasing. The server retains a SHA-256-derived Google subject identifier and the original trial start date, hashed purchase-token ownership records, and bounded access counters. It does not persist Google profile/email data, identity tokens, raw purchase tokens, music or recordings. This record is used for access verification, subscription binding and preventing repeat trials through data clearing/reinstalling; use the same sign-in account to recover access. Different Google accounts remain separate; this does not guarantee one trial per person or device.

An explicit verification sends a Google identity token, Play Integrity verdict token, installation public key and signed request, plus a purchased subscription token when applicable, over HTTPS to the studio licensing backend on Google Cloud. Google processes identity, integrity and Play billing information under its terms. The cloud host processes connection metadata, including IP addresses and request logs; application code uses only short-lived, bounded, in-memory hashed IP buckets for rate limiting and does not log request credentials. Google identity tokens can include email/profile claims transiently during validation, but those claims are not stored in the licensing database. Backend database/function resources are configured for Mumbai when deployed; Google identity, Play and operational services follow their own processing arrangements.

The app stores a server-signed certificate, installation key, protected monotonic clock anchor and retry cooldown privately. These are excluded from song exports and Android Auto Backup. Certificates are reused offline across application restarts on the same device boot. Rebooting or losing a trusted clock anchor requires an explicit online check. Trial certificates retain the original expiry; expired trial results do not automatically poll the server. Paid certificates last at most seven days and never beyond verified entitlement expiry. Refunds or revocations may take up to that interval to remove offline access. Server quotas or connectivity errors can temporarily prevent activation/recovery; existing valid certificates remain usable. No Firebase Authentication session or periodic licensing network poller is used. Google sign-in and direct Play Integrity run only during explicit verification. There is no automatic conversion from demo to paid access.

Licensing identifiers/trial records are retained for access and repeat-trial prevention, separately from local music data. Clearing data or uninstalling does not delete those server records. Local music data is never needed to process a licensing data request.

Licensing data deletion

Request AuraFret licensing data deletion by email

To request deletion of your AuraFret licensing identity and associated server data, email support.djapps@gmail.com with the subject AuraFret licensing data deletion. You can also open this section from Settings → Request licensing data deletion or Subscription options, including after access expires. You do not need to reinstall the app or buy a subscription to send a request.

We verify ownership before processing a request and explain which records are deleted and any limited records retained for security, fraud prevention or applicable obligations. The original trial identifier/date may be retained while necessary to prevent repeat-trial abuse; deleting other licensing data does not promise another free demo. Do not send passwords, payment details or recordings with your request. Google processes its own identity, payment and integrity records under its policies.

A licensing deletion request does not cancel your Google Play subscription, delete your Google account or delete music stored only on your phone. Cancel subscriptions in Google Play. Use AuraFret’s local song/history controls to delete local content, and delete exported backup copies separately. Already issued offline certificates can remain usable until their original expiry.

Restore purchases restores access associated with the Play account, not local songs, recordings or history. The app has no automatic cloud backup. Settings offers explicit saved-song ZIP export and restore. Expiry does not delete local content; saved results, history and deletion remain available. Uninstalling can remove local data but does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel through Google Play, available from Settings and subscription options.

Authorized review access

Authorized evaluators can enter a reusable review code in Subscription options to access the same tools without a purchase or free trial. Only its verified SHA-256 digest is saved in app-private preferences; the entered code is not retained, logged or uploaded. This access has no time or location limit, survives app restart, and can be turned off on the same screen. It is excluded from song backups and removed with app data on uninstall. It does not create, change or cancel a Google Play subscription.

Deletion and control

Users can rename or delete individual practice-history entries, clear practice history after confirmation, discard an unsaved learned-song draft, and delete saved learned songs. Discarding a new draft or deleting a saved learned song also removes its app-owned retained reference recording. Uninstalling the app removes its app-private data according to Android's application-data behavior.

Musical estimates

Chord, key, tempo, beat, likely-next-chord, capo, and strumming outputs are estimates and can be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. This policy describes data handling; it does not guarantee analysis accuracy.
