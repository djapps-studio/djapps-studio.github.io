Your wrist. Your phone.

A small companion
for everyday thoughts.

Capture on your watch without reaching for your phone. With phone Premium active and the devices connected, MemoKeeper saves queued recordings into your phone library. Only confirmed recordings leave the watch queue.

The phone owns your journals and files new watch recordings into its selected journal when they sync. Phone captures and edits stay on the phone. Sync is not a cloud backup.

Personal by design.

Journals live in private app storage. MemoKeeper has no developer-operated journal server and does not save audio recordings.

Speech recognition is handled by your device’s provider and may process audio online. Shared files and plaintext backups are controlled by the destination you choose.

Good to know

A few answers before you begin.

Does MemoKeeper work without a watch?

Yes. Use voice or typed capture on your Android phone. Wear OS is a separate companion app. Watch recording is free, with a queue of 20 unsynced recordings and no automatic overwrite. Phone Premium is required for new sync. Only the phone checks subscription access for its premium features.

Can I use it offline?

Saved journals are stored locally. Speech availability depends on the recognition service and installed languages. Transfers wait for connectivity. Previously verified phone premium access has a bounded offline allowance; a restart or expired allowance can require another Play check.

Does it record audio?

No. MemoKeeper keeps text, not audio recordings. The Android speech provider may use online processing under its own settings and privacy terms.

Is my journal backed up automatically?

No. Create a manual backup on the phone and keep it somewhere safe. Sync, a paired watch and a local emergency restore copy do not replace an off-device backup. Backup files are plaintext.

How do subscriptions and cancellation work?

Watch recording is free and stops at 20 unsynced recordings without overwriting anything. Syncing to the phone, phone creation, editing, favorites and backup restore require Premium. Google Play shows available plans, prices and eligible offers before purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Uninstalling does not cancel it. When access ends, new sync pauses. Reading, sharing, exporting and making a backup of saved journals remain available. Read the User Agreement.

Where can I get help or ask a privacy question?

Open MemoKeeper’s Google Play listing and use the publisher contact under App support. Include your app version and device type. Please do not send private journals, passwords or payment details.
