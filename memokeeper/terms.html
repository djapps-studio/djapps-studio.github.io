MemoKeeper User Agreement

Version 5 | Updated 2 October 2026

Welcome

MemoKeeper helps you keep journals on your Android phone and Wear OS watch. This agreement is between you and the publisher identified on MemoKeeper's Google Play listing. By choosing "I agree to the User Agreement", you accept these terms. You keep ownership of your journal content. Please use MemoKeeper lawfully and respect other people's privacy when using speech capture.

Speech and accuracy

MemoKeeper turns speech into text using an available Android speech-recognition service. It does not save microphone audio or provide audio playback. Transcription can contain mistakes or omissions. Speech, background noise, device capabilities and the recognition service can affect accuracy and availability. Check important names, dates, instructions and other information before relying on them. Speech capture may use a provider's online services, including for a selected recording language; the Privacy Policy explains this further. Phone Settings lets you choose separate phone and watch spoken languages and English-letter output. English-letter output converts recognized script locally to approximate Latin spelling, without translating the meaning; unsupported characters can remain. Available languages, model downloads and mixed-language accuracy depend on each device's speech service. Existing saved entries and typed text are not rewritten.

Your journals and local storage

The phone keeps your journal library in private app storage. The watch shows one active journal. Starting a new journal changes the active journal only after its first entry is saved. Previous watch journals waiting for the phone remain privately stored until the phone confirms their exact revision was saved; then their text is removed from the watch, while small synchronization records remain. Keep the phone copy and a separate backup of important journals. Finish saves available text directly to a journal. Pause retains the current session. Each app may keep one private text draft to recover an interruption; Discard/Cancel clears that draft without creating a journal entry. Recovery opens paused and is best effort: the latest words can be lost, and drafts older than seven days are removed when recovery is checked. No microphone audio is stored. Clearing app data, uninstalling, device failure or device loss can remove access to local information. Keep separate backups of important journals. Deletion is local and does not remove copies on another device or files you shared or exported.

Paired-device sync

Watch journals can sync to the paired phone through Android and Wear OS services. When you finish a new phone capture, MemoKeeper queues that entry to the paired watch; after the watch persists it, the watch sends the resulting complete journal back through the normal durable sync flow. Phone renames, favorites, deletion, later edits to saved entries, and backup restoration are not sent to the watch. Connectivity, device settings and platform services can delay or prevent either direction. A connection indicator or queued message alone does not confirm arrival. Verify important content on the phone; the watch marks a journal synced only after the phone confirms the exact revision was saved. A new phone entry for a completed journal stays on the phone when that journal has already been archived from the watch; it does not reopen watch history. Sync is not a backup service and does not automatically rebuild data removed from either device.

Backup, restore and sharing

You can manually create and restore a MemoKeeper backup from the phone using Android's file picker. You choose a local destination or an installed document provider, which may include Google Drive. MemoKeeper has no automatic or developer-operated cloud backup service. Optional local reminders and the last-backup indicator help you remember to make a manual backup; they do not verify that a provider still retains the file, make backups for you, or guarantee recovery. Backup files are plaintext, not encrypted. Keep them somewhere you trust and check that a backup completed before relying on it.

Restore validates a supported backup and shows a preview before you choose merge or replace. Replace first makes a private emergency copy; that copy is on the same phone and is not protection against device loss or app-data removal. Interrupted operations, unavailable providers, damaged or incompatible files, user deletion and device loss may prevent backup or restoration. A backup may not include changes made after it was created. Share and export create copies controlled by the destination app or provider. Manage those copies separately.
