MemoKeeper Privacy Policy

Version 6 | Updated 2 October 2026

Who provides MemoKeeper

MemoKeeper is provided by the publisher identified on its Google Play listing. For privacy questions, use the publisher contact on that listing. This policy describes the Android phone and Wear OS watch apps. It also explains where an Android service or another app handles information under its own privacy terms.

Information on your devices

MemoKeeper keeps journal text, titles, entry identifiers, timestamps, favorites, deletion markers and synchronization state in private app databases. The apps also store appearance and recording-language/output preferences and watch active-journal/synchronization records. The phone stores the version of the User Agreement you accepted. MemoKeeper does not require a user account and does not include advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. MemoKeeper does not upload journals, transcripts or microphone audio to a developer-operated server. Agreement acceptance is stored locally and is not sent to the publisher.

Google Play billing

MemoKeeper uses Google's Play Billing Library to request available subscription products, localized prices and eligible offers, open Google's purchase dialog, query the current Play account's purchases and acknowledge completed purchases. Google processes payment and account information under its terms and privacy policy. MemoKeeper does not request your payment-card details or require a separate account. Purchase objects and tokens are handled in memory for these operations and are not intentionally logged, included in manual backups, or sent to a developer-operated server. No RevenueCat, Firebase, Supabase or subscription backend is added.

Each app stores minimal local access evidence (whether an eligible purchase was last returned, check time, elapsed device time and boot count) and orientation/acceptance preferences. Obsolete test-build installation-access flags are removed; existing journal data and Play-query evidence are retained. Subscription status is checked again on launch/foreground and during acknowledgement recovery. Failed acknowledgements use Android's durable work scheduler; tokens are re-queried instead of saved in work data. Cached access is bounded to 72 hours on the same device boot after a successful Play check. Google Play may itself return cached information. Subscription access is never restored from a journal backup or trusted from a paired-device message. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes local evidence, but does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Microphone and transcription

Speech capture on the phone or watch uses the microphone when you start or resume a recording session. You can also create phone entries with the keyboard without microphone access. MemoKeeper passes speech to the device's Android recognition service and displays the returned text. MemoKeeper does not write microphone audio to files, keep audio recordings or offer audio playback. For the device-default language it prefers on-device recognition where supported; an explicitly chosen language and the fallback path use the configured system speech service with an offline preference. That request is not a guarantee: the installed service may send audio and related information to its own servers. Processing, retention and network use by that provider follow its settings and privacy terms. English-letter output converts recognized script locally on the recording device to approximate Latin spelling, without a translation service or additional network transfer. Unsupported characters are retained; typed text and existing entries are unchanged. Transcripts are saved when you finish; canceled sessions are not saved to a journal. To help recover an interrupted capture, each app keeps one bounded text draft in private storage excluded from backup. Draft writes are briefly coalesced, so the latest words may still be lost during abrupt termination or storage failure. Recovered text opens paused; recovery does not start the microphone. Finishing or explicitly discarding clears the draft. Drafts older than seven days are rejected and removed when recovery is next checked; this is not a timed secure-erasure guarantee. No audio is stored.

Between your watch and phone

MemoKeeper uses Google Play services and the Wear OS Data Layer to exchange journal entries, complete watch-authoritative journal revisions, metadata, the watch active-journal identifier/title/creation time/selection time, your selected color palette, the watch recording-language/output preference with its revision and random installation identifier, and delivery acknowledgements between your paired devices. Phone light/dark mode and background style stay on the phone; the watch always uses a dark background. A newly finished phone entry and its immutable outgoing snapshot are committed together in private phone storage, then queued to the watch with its stable identifier, text, title, and timestamp. Later phone edits do not change that outgoing snapshot. After the watch accepts the handoff or confirms the journal is archived, the phone removes the outgoing text and title and retains small identifiers, timestamps and a text hash for retry and merge safety. A failed connection leaves saved text and its pending handoff available for retry. After the watch persists it, the watch sends the resulting complete journal revision to the phone through the normal durable sync flow. Phone renames, favorites, deletion, later edits to saved entries, and backup restoration are not sent to the watch. If a newly captured phone entry targets a journal already archived from the watch, it stays in the phone journal and does not restore that journal to the watch. Google and Android manage connectivity and transport under their own service terms. MemoKeeper does not operate a synchronization server, and this feature is not a cloud backup service.

Optional reminders and shortcuts

You may enable local journal or backup reminders and choose an hour between 08:00 and 21:00. These are off initially, use Android notification permission, and contain generic prompts rather than journal text. Android may delay or suppress reminders. MemoKeeper stores your reminder choices, last notification times, completed-capture count and last successful manual-backup time locally. Reminders never create a backup automatically. An optional phone home-screen widget opens voice or typed capture and shows no journal content.

Export, share and manual backup

Choosing Copy entry places the selected text on Android's clipboard. MemoKeeper marks it as sensitive to suppress content previews on supporting Android versions. Clipboard retention and access by the operating system or another app follow that platform's behavior; copying is not an encrypted export.

Sharing prepares temporary text attachments in the phone's private cache and grants the chosen receiving app access to the selected files. Attachments older than 24 hours are removed when another share is prepared; Android can clear cache earlier. The receiving app controls any copies it keeps.

Exports create text, Markdown or ZIP files at the destination you choose. Manual MemoKeeper backups include journals and selected preferences, but not device-lock credentials, agreement acceptance or recording-language/output preferences. Backups are checksummed for integrity and are plaintext, not encrypted. Android's file picker may offer local storage or installed providers such as Google Drive. MemoKeeper does not sign you into Drive or request access to your entire drive. The chosen provider controls its copy and any network transfer. No automatic backup is provided; the apps disable Android automatic app backup and exclude journal databases and files from device transfer.

Restoration reads and validates the file you select, previews it, and merges or replaces phone journals. Before replace, MemoKeeper writes an emergency backup in private phone storage. It keeps the latest emergency copy until a later replacement overwrites it or app storage is removed. This is not an off-device recovery service. Remove shared, exported and backup files through their destination apps or providers when you no longer want them.

Retention and deletion

The phone retains the journal library in private app storage until you remove or replace that storage. The watch shows one active journal. When a new journal becomes active, previous journals are hidden but their text stays privately stored while delivery is pending, including when offline. The watch removes completed journal text only after the phone confirms the exact revision was saved. It retains small journal identifiers, checksums, timestamps, import markers, and hashed phone-entry receipt records so retries and late messages cannot duplicate entries or reopen completed journals. This content removal is not a secure-erasure guarantee. Deleting a journal in MemoKeeper hides it locally; journal content and deletion markers can remain in the database to preserve synchronization behavior. Deletion does not erase the other device's copy, and a newer watch revision can bring back a phone journal that was locally removed. No synchronization operation deletes your exported or backup files. Clearing app storage or uninstalling normally removes private journals, preferences, cache and emergency backups on that device. Copies held by another device, recipient or document provider must be managed there separately.

Protection and choices

Android's app sandbox and device protections apply to private storage. MemoKeeper does not add its own encryption to its databases or backup files, and a checksum is not encryption. You can optionally require your phone's device credential to open journal content and block screenshots and recent-app previews. MemoKeeper does not receive or store your PIN, pattern, password or biometric data. These options do not protect exported files or prevent privileged access to the device.

You control microphone permission in Android settings. Denying permission prevents speech capture; other supported text and journal features remain available. You choose when to share, export or back up information and which destination receives it.

Public website

The MemoKeeper information and policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, advertising, cookies, contact forms or journal uploads to these pages. GitHub receives connection information needed to serve the website and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following a Google Play link opens Google's service under its own terms. The website cannot access your app journals.

Policy updates

The private text-draft recovery, durable phone handoff queue, optional local reminders, backup-health information, capture shortcuts and public website described in version 5 remain. Future changes to data handling require a policy review and any notice or consent required by applicable law.

For support or privacy questions, use the publisher contact under App support on MemoKeeper’s Google Play listing.
