Delete your WristScribe licensing account

These instructions apply to the Google-account licensing revision. If your installed version lacks this control, you can still request help by email below.

On your phone, open WristScribe → Settings → Premium → Delete licensing account. Sign in with the same Google account to prove ownership, then complete the deletion. This control stays available after the demo or subscription access expires. You can reinstall the app and use it without restoring journals or buying a subscription. Account deletion does not require a successful device-integrity verdict.

You can submit a deletion request without reinstalling the app. Email support.djapps@gmail.com with the subject “WristScribe licensing account deletion”. Support must verify ownership before acting; an email claim alone is not proof. Never send passwords, identity tokens, purchase tokens or journal contents. The normal ownership-verification route uses explicit Google sign-in in the app with a fresh server challenge.

What is removed and retained

Ordinary licensing-account and installation records are removed. The minimal service schema does not store names, email addresses, profiles or installation lists. App-scoped keyed account hashes, original demo start/expiry and purchase-ownership hashes remain for the licensing service’s lifetime solely to prevent trial resets and purchase reassignment. These are pseudonymous records, not anonymous data. Admission counters and nonce hashes target seven-day asynchronous cleanup. Operational/audit logs follow the retention described in the Privacy Policy.

Separate actions

Deleting this account does not cancel Google Play subscriptions, delete local journals, remove exports held by another app or immediately invalidate certificates on other offline installations. Cancel payments in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Delete journals in WristScribe and manage exported copies at their destinations. Uninstalling or clearing app storage normally removes local journals; make a manual backup first if needed.
