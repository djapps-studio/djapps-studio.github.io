WristScribe User Agreement

Version 10 | Updated 16 October 2026

WristScribe: Voice Journal was previously called MemoKeeper. Version 10 introduces an explicitly activated Google-account demo and server-verified phone licensing. This version applies when presented and accepted in the app. Available access features depend on the installed app release.

Welcome

WristScribe helps you keep journals on your Android phone and Wear OS watch. This agreement is between you and the publisher identified on WristScribe's Google Play listing. By choosing "I agree to the User Agreement", you accept these terms. You keep ownership of your journal content. Please use WristScribe lawfully and respect other people's privacy when using speech capture.

Speech and accuracy

WristScribe turns speech into text using an available Android speech-recognition service. It does not save microphone audio or provide audio playback. Transcription can contain mistakes or omissions. Speech, background noise, device capabilities and the recognition service can affect accuracy and availability. Check important names, dates, instructions and other information before relying on them. Speech capture may use a provider's online services, including for a selected recording language; the Privacy Policy explains this further. Phone Settings lets you choose separate phone and watch spoken languages and English-letter output. English-letter output converts recognized script locally to approximate Latin spelling, without translating the meaning; unsupported characters can remain. Available languages, model downloads and mixed-language accuracy depend on each device's speech service. Existing saved entries and typed text are not rewritten.

Your journals and local storage

The phone maintains all journals and journal creation. The watch keeps up to 20 unsynced text recordings and shows the latest pending recording. At capacity it stops accepting new recordings; it never automatically overwrites the oldest one. Transfers waiting for confirmation still occupy slots. A slot becomes free only after the phone confirms that exact recording was saved. Older unsynced content retained during an upgrade is preserved even if it exceeds this limit; new capture stays blocked until capacity is available.

Finish saves the visible text. Pause retains the session. Each app may keep one private recovery draft; Cancel/Discard clears that draft without creating an entry. Recovery is best effort and opens paused; recent words can be lost, and drafts older than seven days are removed when checked. No microphone audio is stored. Clearing app data, uninstalling, device failure or loss can remove local information. Keep separate backups of important journals.

Paired-device sync

New watch recordings sync through the Wear OS Data Layer only while phone Premium access is available. The phone assigns a recording to its selected journal when it first receives it; changing the selection before sync changes the destination of recordings still waiting. If no selected journal is available, the phone uses an available recent journal or creates one. A full journal can roll over into a new phone journal. Choose the destination in the phone journal using Use for incoming watch recordings. Phone captures and journal edits are local; they do not require a round trip through the watch.

Subscription expiry pauses new recording receipt. The watch can still capture until its 20-record queue is full. Reconnect with phone Premium active to resume sync. An acknowledgement for content already committed on the phone may still clear its matching watch slot after access ends. Connectivity, platform services and subscription checks can delay transfer. A Data Layer write or connection indicator does not prove delivery. Exact recording checksums and durable receipts prevent duplicate retries, including after phone edits or local deletion. Older whole-journal transfers and phone handoffs are retained only for upgrade recovery and use their original compatibility rules. Sync is not backup and does not rebuild deleted data.

Backup, restore and sharing

You can manually create and restore a WristScribe backup from the phone using Android's file picker. You choose a local destination or an installed document provider, which may include Google Drive. WristScribe has no automatic or developer-operated cloud backup service. Optional local reminders and the last-backup indicator help you remember to make a manual backup; they do not verify that a provider still retains the file, make backups for you, or guarantee recovery. Backup files are plaintext, not encrypted. Keep them somewhere you trust and check that a backup completed before relying on it.

Restore validates a supported backup and shows a preview before you choose merge or replace. Replace first makes a private emergency copy; that copy is on the same phone and is not protection against device loss or app-data removal. Interrupted operations, unavailable providers, damaged or incompatible files, user deletion and device loss may prevent backup or restoration. A backup may not include changes made after it was created. Share and export create copies controlled by the destination app or provider. Manage those copies separately.

Phone demo and Google Play subscriptions

Watch recording and latest-pending viewing remain free, with the existing 20-unsynced-recording limit. Phone Premium permits new recording receipt, creation, appending, renaming, editing, favorites and backup restore. Reading, searching, sharing, exporting, making backups, local deletion, privacy controls and licensing-account management remain available after access expires. Expiry preserves journals and pending watch recordings; exact already-committed duplicates may still be acknowledged.

Choose Sign in with Google to activate or recover 30 days of full phone access without payment details or a subscription. The licensing server establishes one immutable start and expiry per Google account for this app. Reinstalling or clearing local storage does not restart that account's demo; explicit sign-in recovers its original expiry. Journal recovery is separate and requires a backup. Different Google accounts have separate trials; this is not a one-trial-per-person guarantee or protection against every modified/rooted device. The demo never automatically becomes a paid subscription.

Ordinary launches and feature use verify a server-signed certificate locally, bound to this installation's Android Keystore key. Activation/recovery, purchase verification/restoration, expired paid leases and an untrusted clock require an explicit online action. After reboot, if the remaining duration cannot be trusted, online verification is required. Expired demo results remain local rather than triggering repeated server checks. Network errors or rate limits can temporarily prevent verification and premium operations; they do not erase journals. There is no background sign-in session or scheduled licensing polling.

Paid monthly and yearly base plans retain their existing Google Play identifiers, displayed prices and renewal terms. Subscribe only through the explicit Google Play purchase dialog. New purchases are bound to the selected licensing account. After checkout, choose Verify or restore purchase to finish server verification if needed. Existing purchases without a licensing-account binding require support ownership review; they are never silently reassigned to another Google account. Google controls payment, renewal and cancellation. Existing paid subscribers are not cancelled by this update.

Paid certificates last at most seven days and never beyond the entitlement expiry verified by the server. A refund, hold or revocation may therefore take up to the remaining lease duration to end offline access; explicit successful verification can reflect the change earlier. Cancellation of renewal does not end a still-valid paid period. Cancel through Google Play > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions. Uninstalling, signing out, deleting a licensing account or deleting journals does not cancel payments.

For Google Play review, the publisher may provide the existing separate local reviewer code. It grants review access without creating a purchase, subscription or trial, can be removed on Premium, and is excluded from journal backups and device transfer. It is not a server security boundary. Wear has no purchase, sign-in, code or entitlement screen.

Licensing-account deletion is available from phone Premium even without active access. Explicit Google sign-in with a fresh request nonce verifies ownership; a new installation can use this flow without restoring journals. Instructions and support are available at https://djapps-studio.github.io/wristscribe/delete-account.html and support.djapps@gmail.com. Ordinary account/installation records are removed on verified deletion. The service retains app-scoped keyed account hashes, original demo dates and purchase-ownership hashes for its lifetime solely to prevent trial resets and purchase reassignment. These pseudonymous records are not anonymous. Previously issued certificates on other offline installations can remain usable until their original expiry. Local journals, recipient copies and Google Play subscriptions must be managed separately.

App updates

Both apps can check a public release list over HTTPS while you use a non-recording screen. Automatic checks are enabled by default, normally at most daily, and can be turned off under App updates in phone Settings > About & Legal or watch Connection > Guide and legal. Manual checking remains available. The Privacy Policy explains the website connection and locally stored update preferences. A notice is not proof that a release is available to your Google Play account or region. Choose Open Google Play to check availability and install through Google Play. WristScribe does not silently install updates, force an update during recording or replace your saved journals. Google Play's separate auto-update setting remains under your control.
