A CONVERSATION THAT STAYS CLOSE.

Your people.
Your shared moments.

01 / CONNECT

Nearby conversations.

Direct messages use Bluetooth and Wi-Fi between approved phones. Both phones must keep YoMilo open. Range varies; messages do not relay through strangers.

02 / SHARE

More than a message.

Share photos, files and recorded voice notes. Bring your circle together with groups, polls, events, statuses and search.

03 / TRUST

Know who is there.

Approve connections and compare codes. Verify safety numbers independently; a nickname alone does not prove identity. Chats and attachments use encrypted local storage.

Live voice calls and location sharing are unavailable in this version. Microphone access is used for voice notes only.

Sign in with Google to activate 30 days of full access. No payment details or subscription are required, and the demo never automatically becomes paid. Server time fixes one original expiry per Google account for YoMilo. Reinstalling with the same account recovers that expiry; different accounts remain separate.

Regular monthly or annual plans

After the demo, choose a regular Google Play plan to continue premium operations. Google Play shows the localized recurring price before purchase. Paid plans charge when subscribed and automatically renew until cancelled in Google Play.

Valid signed certificates work offline. App restarts retain trusted time in the same device boot; reboot or an untrusted clock requires explicit online verification. Paid leases last at most seven days and never beyond verified entitlement expiry. Offline refunds/revocations can take up to seven days to affect access.

Expiry preserves local history. Read-only viewing, eligible encrypted backup export, deletion, privacy and account management remain available. Uninstalling destroys local chats/identity unless you separately exported an eligible backup; account recovery does not restore them.

New name. Same nearby app.

YoMilo is the new name of NearChat. Your existing chats, identity and Google Play subscription carry over. Renaming the app does not start another free trial.

Your history stays yours.

There is no developer-operated messaging server, advertising or analytics service. Google Play services and Billing have their own data practices. Cancelling does not delete local history. Deleting local data or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.
