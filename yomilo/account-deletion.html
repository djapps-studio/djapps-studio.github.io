YoMilo licensing account deletion

Version notice: The licensing features described here are prepared for YoMilo 0.8.9 and are not yet enabled in the public release. Availability depends on the installed app version. Contact support if your version does not show these controls.

Prepared for YoMilo 0.8.9 by CodeFoundry · DJ Apps Studio. The support request route below is available even if your installed app does not yet include licensing controls.

Request deletion, including after access expires

Open YoMilo's access/account screen and choose Request licensing account deletion. Sign in with the Google account you used for licensing to verify ownership. No paid plan or trial activation is needed for this request. If you reinstalled, use the same Google account. You can send the request even when the trial or paid access has ended.

You can also email support.djapps@gmail.com to request licensing-account deletion. Include YoMilo, your request, and the licensing support ID displayed in the app if available. Do not send passwords, Google identity tokens, private keys, full purchase tokens or private conversations. Contact support if you cannot open the app or access the original Google account.

Ownership verification and processing

Support will normally ask you to complete the in-app Google sign-in deletion request, which verifies account ownership without exposing tokens by email. If you lost the device, reinstalling and signing in with the original account can verify it. If that is unavailable, support explains the additional verification or Google account/Play support escalation needed. Email control, an order screenshot or knowledge of a purchase token alone is not enough to assign a subscription to another account. Requests are normally processed within 30 days.

What is deleted and retained

Optional licensing-account/support data and request metadata are removed when no longer needed. Minimal pseudonymous original trial dates and keyed purchase-ownership records remain for the service lifetime to prevent trial resets and purchase reassignment. Those records contain no Google email/name, raw identity/purchase tokens, chats or private keys. Short-lived admission counters have a three-day cleanup period; platform logs have separate disclosed retention.

Separate controls

This request does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage cancellation in Google Play. It does not erase local chats, identity or exported files. Use YoMilo's Delete all local data and identity control for local deletion, and delete exported backups/Downloads separately. Other recipients and Google independently control their copies/data.
