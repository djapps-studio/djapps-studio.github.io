YoMilo Subscriptions

YoMilo subscriptions and offline access · 8 October 2026

A subscription is required for app use. Monthly and annual plans provide the same available app features. Each supports the Google-managed 15-day offer for eligible new subscribers. Google decides eligibility across the app; changing plans or reinstalling does not guarantee another trial.

The trial starts when Google Play confirms the subscription. After 15 days, your chosen monthly or annual plan charges the full displayed recurring price and automatically renews until cancelled. Cancel in Google Play before trial end to avoid the first charge. A no-trial offer charges on subscription. Prices come from Google Play; the app has no hardcoded live prices.

Plan availability and trial eligibility are determined by Google Play. No trial starts and no app access is granted merely by installing YoMilo. Pending payment does not grant access. Restore using the purchasing Play account. Cancellation can preserve access until the current period ends; Play determines ownership.

A confirmed ownership check permits up to 24 hours of offline app use during the same device boot. App restarts retain the encrypted check. After 24 hours or a device reboot, connect to Google Play and retry. If Play confirms no qualifying purchase, access ends immediately. Your local chats, files and identity are retained when access ends.

Manage or cancel: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions
Direct YoMilo management: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions?sku=nearchat_plus&package=com.codefoundry.nearchat

Deleting local data or uninstalling is separate from cancelling a subscription. Local data deletion remains available without paid access. If a subscription needs payment or account attention, use Google Play support; do not email card details. YoMilo support: support.djapps@gmail.com.

Live voice calls and location sharing are disabled. Voice-note attachments remain available with access. Legal documents are available offline in the app and online at https://djapps-studio.github.io/yomilo/.

Manage or cancel in Google Play ↗

Authorised app reviewers can use a separately issued, digitally signed review credential. This grants the same available features without a purchase or free trial, does not create Play ownership, and is labelled as review access. It does not provide access to another user's data. The credential is stored only in authenticated encrypted, app-private no-backup storage and is removed by Remove review access, local data deletion or uninstalling. No review sign-in account or messaging backend is created.

YoMilo is the new name of NearChat. The Android package remains com.codefoundry.nearchat; existing local identity, history and Google Play subscriptions continue with the same app. This branding update does not start another free trial.
