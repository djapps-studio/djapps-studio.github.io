YoMilo Subscriptions

Version notice: The licensing features described here are prepared for YoMilo 0.8.9 and are not yet enabled in the public release. Availability depends on the installed app version. Contact support if your version does not show these controls.

YoMilo trial, paid plans and offline access - 0.8.9

Sign in with Google to activate or recover 30 days of full access. No payment details or subscription are needed; the demo never becomes paid automatically. Server time fixes one start and expiry per Google account for this app. Reinstalling and signing into the same account recovers the original expiry. Different Google accounts remain separate; this is not one trial per person or unbreakable protection on modified devices. Licensing recovery does not recover deleted chat history or identity.

Ordinary launches and features reuse a valid installation-bound signed certificate locally, without sign-in, licensing HTTP calls or Integrity checks. App restarts keep a protected monotonic clock anchor in the same boot. Reboot or missing/invalid time evidence requires explicit online verification. Wall-clock rollback does not extend access. Expired-trial results are saved locally; reopening does not poll the server. Temporary quotas or network failures grant no extension.

After the demo, choose a regular monthly or annual Google Play base plan. Plans show the recurring localized price, charge when subscribed, and automatically renew until cancelled in Google Play. The separate demo never starts this subscription. Existing Play subscription/trial terms are preserved. New purchases bind to the licensing account. Restore using the original Google licensing and Play accounts. Existing unbound subscriptions may need verified support migration; do not purchase again to solve a migration problem.

Paid certificates last at most seven days and never beyond actual verified entitlement expiry. Refund/revocation detection while offline can lag by up to seven days. Choose Restore purchases to renew an expired paid lease online. Pending, held, paused or expired purchases do not grant paid access. No background licensing refresh, scheduled polling, Firebase Authentication or App Check session is introduced. Google Play app-update checks are separate.

Expiry stops premium operations and preserves local data. View and export retained data opens read-only history and encrypted backup tools; disappearing/view-once content is excluded. Local deletion, account management, legal/privacy documents and subscription management remain accessible.

Request licensing account deletion from the access screen using fresh Google sign-in, or use https://djapps-studio.github.io/yomilo/account-deletion.html / support.djapps@gmail.com. Support normally processes ownership-verified requests within 30 days. Minimal pseudonymous original trial dates and purchase ownership hashes remain for the service lifetime to prevent trial resets and purchase transfer. See the Privacy Policy. Account deletion does not cancel Google Play billing, erase local data or remove exported files. Those require separate actions. Never send passwords, private keys, identity tokens, full purchase tokens or chats to support.

Authorized reviewers retain separately issued visible signed review access. No purchase is started by a review credential.
