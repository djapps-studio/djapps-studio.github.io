YoMilo User Agreement - Version 6 - prepared 16 October 2026 for version 0.8.9

YoMilo is the new name of NearChat. The Android package remains com.codefoundry.nearchat; existing local identity, history and Google Play subscriptions continue with the same app. This branding update does not start another free trial.

YoMilo is provided by CodeFoundry · DJ Apps Studio. Privacy, support and complaints: support.djapps@gmail.com.

1. Age and nearby communication
YoMilo is intended for adults aged 18 and over. You must be at least 18 to use it.
YoMilo provides communication with approved people nearby. Delivery is foreground by default, with an optional, explicitly started 15-minute nearby session and persistent Stop notification; app lock must be off for that session. Both phones need compatible YoMilo builds, Google Play services, enabled Bluetooth/Wi-Fi, appropriate permissions and valid trial, paid or authorized review access. Range and delivery depend on devices and surroundings. Messages do not relay through strangers. YoMilo is not an emergency communication service. Live voice calls, GPS/assisted location, live location and the dedicated place-sharing feature are disabled in version 0.8.9. Voice-note attachments remain available.

2. Independent 30-day trial and paid subscriptions
Sign in explicitly with Google to activate 30 days of full access using server time. No subscription, payment details or Google Play subscription trial is required. The demo never automatically becomes paid. One original trial start and expiry is kept per Google account for this app. Clearing app data or reinstalling and signing into the same account recovers that original expiry; local chat history and signing identity are not recovered by licensing. Different Google accounts remain separate. This is not one trial per person or a guarantee against modified/rooted devices.

After the demo expires, you may choose the regular monthly or annual Google Play base plan. Both include the same available premium features. Google Play displays the full localized recurring price and billing period before confirmation. A paid plan charges when you subscribe and automatically renews until cancelled. Cancel in Google Play before the next renewal to avoid that renewal. The independent demo does not require cancellation. Existing Google-managed trials/subscriptions remain subject to their original Play terms and are not reset or cancelled by this update.

Pending, paused, suspended, held or expired purchases do not create paid access. Renewal cancellation can leave access until the actual verified entitlement expiry. New purchases are bound to the licensing account; restore using the original licensing account and Google Play account. Existing unbound purchases require verified migration through support and must not be reassigned between accounts. If migration is needed, contact support rather than purchasing again. Applicable Google Play refund/cancellation rules apply without limiting statutory rights. Deleting data, a licensing account or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.

3. Offline access and access loss
The server issues an installation-bound signed certificate. Ordinary app launches and premium operations reuse a valid certificate locally without Google sign-in, Play Integrity or licensing-server calls. The trial certificate ends at the original account expiry. Paid certificates last at most seven days and never beyond the server-verified paid entitlement expiry. Refund or revocation may therefore take up to seven days to affect offline access. Expired paid leases require explicit online restore/verification. There is no background licensing refresh or scheduled poll.

A protected monotonic clock anchor preserves trusted time across app restarts in the same device boot. Wall-clock rollback cannot extend access. Reboot, missing/tampered evidence or an untrusted clock anchor requires explicit online verification; the app does not guess remaining time from the wall clock. Network failure or service quotas can prevent verification, and no extra access is granted while it fails. Signed expired-trial outcomes are retained locally without repeated launch-time polling.

Expiry locks premium communication/creation operations and preserves local data. Read-only retained history, eligible encrypted history export, local deletion, privacy/legal documents, subscription management and licensing-account deletion/support remain available. Disappearing/view-once content is excluded from archive viewing/export. To request licensing-account deletion, use the access screen or the public deletion page and support contact. Fresh Google sign-in verifies ownership even after expiry. Processing is normally within 30 days. Minimal pseudonymous trial dates and purchase-ownership hashes remain for the service lifetime to prevent trial resets and purchase reassignment; see the Privacy Policy. Subscription cancellation, local deletion and exported-file deletion are separate actions.

Authorised app reviewers can use a separately issued, digitally signed review credential. This grants the same available features without a purchase or free trial, does not create Play ownership, and is labelled as review access. It does not provide access to another user's data. The credential is stored only in authenticated encrypted, app-private no-backup storage and is removed by Remove review access, local data deletion or uninstalling. No review sign-in account or messaging backend is created.

4. Your identity, data and conduct
Approve your contacts, compare connection codes and verify safety numbers independently. A nickname is not proof of identity. Keep your device secure. Do not share abusive, unlawful, infringing or non-consensual content. Recipients control delivered copies; deletion and expiry cannot guarantee erasure from their devices, screenshots, exports or recordings.

Messages, files and identities are stored on your device. Uninstalling or deleting local data permanently destroys local history and identity continuity. No identity backup or recovery is provided. The Privacy Policy explains local processing, third-party services, retention and deletion. The developer does not operate a chat server or remotely retrieve your conversations.

5. Google Play updates
YoMilo automatically checks for compatible updates from Google Play while in the foreground. You can defer an update or approve its download. A downloaded update asks you to restart; active nearby connections end during restart while saved local data remains. Updates require Google Play availability and internet connectivity.

6. Acceptance and changes
Read the Privacy Policy and this agreement before activation or subscribing. Acceptance is recorded locally by version and is separate from Play ownership and chat identity. Updated material terms may require renewed acceptance. No security-audit certification or guaranteed radio range/delivery is claimed.

Encrypted history files and optional avatars
You may opt in to share a bundled avatar with approved contacts. Offline copies can persist until reconnecting. You can create a passphrase-protected history backup in a file you choose. Restore opens a separate read-only archive; signing identity and contact trust are not transferred. View-once media, disappearing messages and statuses are excluded. Keep the passphrase safe: it cannot be reset. Delete exported backups separately from app data.

Saving an archived file to Downloads creates an unencrypted copy that other apps may access. That copy remains after the archive closes or app data is deleted. Delete exported Downloads files separately.
