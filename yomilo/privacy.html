YoMilo Privacy Policy

Version notice: The licensing features described here are prepared for YoMilo 0.8.9 and are not yet enabled in the public release. Availability depends on the installed app version. Contact support if your version does not show these controls.

YoMilo is the new name of NearChat. The Android package remains com.codefoundry.nearchat; existing local identity, history and Google Play subscriptions continue with the same app. This branding update does not start another free trial.

Policy version: 7, prepared 20 October 2026 for the licensing directory update
App: YoMilo (com.codefoundry.nearchat), version 0.8.9
Developer: CodeFoundry · DJ Apps Studio
Privacy and support contact: support.djapps@gmail.com

YoMilo connects approved nearby Android devices over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and related device-to-device technologies. CodeFoundry does not operate a messaging server and does not receive or store your conversations. An explicitly activated 30-day Google-account trial, verified paid subscription or authorized review licence enables premium operations. Nearby messaging itself does not require internet while licensed. Deliberate activation/recovery, purchase/restore and untrusted-clock verification require internet. The developer operates a separate licensing server, which receives no conversation content or chat identity keys.

1. Data used on your device

You choose a public nickname and create an Android Keystore cryptographic identity. Your nickname is advertised only when you enable nearby visibility. You can opt in to share a bundled avatar with approved contacts over an authenticated connection. Sharing is off by default; disabling it asks connected contacts to remove the avatar, while offline copies may remain until reconnecting. Private identity keys are not designed to leave the device. Contacts, fingerprints, messages, groups, polls, events, statuses, drafts, preferences and delivery/read metadata are stored locally in encrypted SQLCipher storage.

Files and media you explicitly select are imported into encrypted app-private storage and sent to recipients you approve. YoMilo does not request broad photo-library, file-system or address-book access. A system contact picker supplies only the selected contact detail. Original media can contain embedded location or other metadata; supported photo re-encoding can remove EXIF.

The microphone is used only for voice notes after your action and Android permission. Voice notes are encrypted attachments. Live voice calls and location sharing are disabled in this release. YoMilo does not acquire GPS or assisted-location fixes. Android 10–12L can still require foreground location permission and Location services for nearby discovery; the app does not use that discovery permission to read coordinates. Android 13+ uses nearby-device permissions instead.

Older installations may contain location messages or call-history metadata created before these features were disabled. Disabling the features does not silently erase existing history or restore live sharing. You can remove local data using the app's deletion controls. A place or address typed as an ordinary message remains ordinary message content.

Android performs optional biometric/device-credential authentication; YoMilo receives the result, not biometric templates. Notifications and group-event reminders are local. Message previews are hidden by default. There is no remote push service.

2. Sharing and third-party services

Messages, files and voice notes go directly to approved contacts or group members. Recipients can retain, export, photograph or record their copies. Local deletion cannot guarantee removal from another device.

YoMilo uses Google Play services Nearby Connections. Google documents usage analytics such as connection/discovery performance, device model, country, build version and app package name. These Google-controlled diagnostics are separate from developer analytics; users can review Android Settings > Google > Usage & diagnostics. See Nearby Connections and Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing handles regular plans, prices, payments, subscription state and acknowledgement. No payment-card details reach YoMilo. For explicit activation/recovery, purchase/restore or licensing-account deletion, Credential Manager obtains a Google identity token bound to the request nonce. Our server verifies its signature, audience and expiry. It uses a server-secret keyed hash of Google account subject and app ID as the licensing identifier. We do not persist the raw subject, identity token, Google name or photo. The verified Google email is retained only for the disclosed licensing support directory after updated agreement acceptance, an explicit updated-client verification and server enablement, as explained below. No Firebase Authentication/App Check user session or background sign-in is introduced.

The licensing server validates installation-key proof and request-bound Standard Play Integrity checks for activation/purchases. Google processes app/device/licensing security signals. The server also sends purchase tokens to Google's Publisher API to verify product, account ownership, subscription state and actual expiry, and acknowledges valid purchases. Raw identity, Integrity and purchase tokens remain transient; they are not written to the licensing database or application logs. The server stores only the minimum pseudonymous account ID, immutable trial start/expiry, purchase-ownership hashes and admission counters/request hashes. No conversation content, nickname, contact fingerprints, proximity history, location or chat private key is sent to licensing.

The per-installation private signing key stays in Android Keystore. Its public key/digest binds the signed certificate to that installation; it is not a device-wide identifier or a one-person limit. The app stores its certificate, protected monotonic time/boot anchor and retry cooldown in authenticated encrypted no-backup storage. Trial expiry is exactly 30 days after first server activation for the Google account and this app. Different accounts remain separate. Reinstalling with the same account recovers the original trial dates, not lost local chats/identity. Paid certificates last at most seven days and never past verified entitlement expiry, so offline refunds/revocations can take up to seven days to take effect. Ordinary launches/features use local certificates without sign-in, Integrity or server polling. Reboot or untrusted clock evidence requires explicit online verification. The old client-only Play cache no longer grants production access.

The proposed dedicated Google Cloud project uses Mumbai for the licensing compute/database where supported. Google identity, Play, support and control-plane processing can occur elsewhere; this is not an India-only processing promise. Network addresses/request metadata may appear in platform operational logs. Our configured default operational log retention is intended to be seven days; mandatory audit/platform logs may have different retention. We do not log request bodies, raw identity/purchase tokens, private keys or user content. Daily admission records expire after three days with asynchronous TTL cleanup. Server quotas/rate limits can temporarily prevent activation without extending a trial or subscription.

Camera apps, document/media providers and export destinations receive data when you initiate their corresponding actions. Their storage, backups and policies apply. YoMilo has no developer-operated advertising, analytics, cloud-backup or crash-reporting service. CodeFoundry does not sell personal information.

YoMilo checks for app updates through Google Play. Google processes device metadata, the installed app version and installed module/asset-pack information to determine update availability and download size. YoMilo does not send chats, identities or location to the update service. Update prompts can be deferred; Google Play handles the download and installation. See Google Play in-app updates and data safety. A deferred-update version and reminder time are stored locally.

Authorised app reviewers can use a separately issued, digitally signed review credential. This grants the same available features without a purchase or free trial, does not create Play ownership, and is labelled as review access. It does not provide access to another user's data. The credential is stored only in authenticated encrypted, app-private no-backup storage and is removed by Remove review access, local data deletion or uninstalling. No review sign-in account or messaging backend is created.

3. Security

Approved peers use mutually authenticated encrypted sessions. Users should compare connection codes and independently verify safety numbers; a nickname is not identity proof. Local conversations and attachments are encrypted with keys protected by Android Keystore where supported. Android backups of app data are disabled. Capture and decrypted previews stop when the app leaves the foreground. Nearby delivery normally stops too, unless you explicitly start a 15-minute nearby session while the app is open and unlocked. That session has a persistent Stop notification, requires app lock to be off, and stops on expiry, subscription access loss, process death or removal from Recents. It does not restart at boot; Android may interrupt it. It uses no microphone or cloud messaging.

No security measure eliminates every risk, particularly on a compromised device or when a recipient retains content. Security review and physical device acceptance remain release requirements; no independent audit certification is claimed.

4. Retention, deletion and subscription changes

Local content remains until you delete it, uninstall the app or a supported expiry rule removes it. Use Settings > Delete all local data and identity to remove chats, contacts, private attachments, preferences, cached licensing certificates and clock/cooldown records, any review credential and the local cryptographic identity. This control also remains available on the subscription screen when access is unavailable. Deletion and uninstalling destroy identity continuity. Optional encrypted history backups are files you explicitly create and protect with a passphrase. They include retained chats, groups and available media, excluding view-once media, disappearing messages and statuses. Restoring opens an isolated read-only archive without replacing live history, transferring signing keys or restoring contact trust. The developer cannot recover a lost passphrase. Backup files and copies held by a selected storage provider are outside app deletion; delete those separately. Choosing a cloud document provider can store your encrypted file with that provider, but YoMilo operates no cloud backup service.

You can explicitly save a restored archive's file as an unencrypted copy in Downloads/YoMilo. Other apps may access that copy. Closing the archive or deleting app data does not remove exported Downloads files; delete them separately.

Subscription cancellation, expiry, pending payment or failed checking does not erase local history. Premium operations require valid access; retained read-only history, eligible encrypted backup export, deletion, legal/privacy and account management remain available. CodeFoundry cannot remotely retrieve or erase data held only on your device. Delivered/exported copies and Google's separately processed information are outside this local deletion operation.

Licensing-account deletion: use the access screen's Request licensing account deletion control, which requires fresh Google sign-in and installation proof without a paid plan or Integrity verdict. This records an ownership-verified request. You may also use https://djapps-studio.github.io/yomilo/account-deletion.html or email support.djapps@gmail.com with the app name and displayed licensing support ID if available. Do not send passwords, identity tokens, private keys, full purchase tokens or private chats. Support will explain ownership verification (normally fresh in-app Google sign-in after reinstall if needed) and escalation if you cannot access the original account. An email claim alone cannot identify a Google subject or transfer a purchase.

We normally process requests within 30 days, removing optional account/support data and request metadata when no longer needed. We retain only the pseudonymous account/trial dates and keyed purchase-ownership mappings for the service lifetime to preserve the original one-per-account trial and prevent token reassignment. These minimal anti-abuse tombstones contain no email, name, raw tokens or conversations; deleting an account does not reset its trial. Voluntarily supplied support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and applicable obligations. This retention and the new policy require owner approval before production rollout.

Deleting a licensing account, local data or uninstalling does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel in Google Play separately. Local deletion does not submit a server-account request, and an account request does not erase local/exported files or other people's copies. Google independently processes identity/Play data under its policies.

5. Children and other people's data

YoMilo is intended for adults aged 18 and over, matching its Google Play target audience. It is not directed to children. If you are under 18, do not use YoMilo. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact support.djapps@gmail.com; avoid including private conversations or payment details.

Only share another person's information or media when you have permission or another lawful basis. Do not send unlawful, abusive or non-consensual content.

6. Contact and changes

For privacy questions, support or complaints, email support.djapps@gmail.com with the app name. Do not include passwords, payment details or private conversations in your initial message. Any further information should be limited to what is needed for the request.

We may update this policy when features, processing or applicable obligations change. Material changes will be disclosed through the app or its store listing. The effective date will be revised.

Verified licensing support directory — version 7

After renewed agreement acceptance and an explicit successful updated-client verification, the publisher can retain the verified Google sign-in email, app-specific Support ID, directory creation/last-verification times, original trial dates and a limited dated purchase snapshot: product, plan, subscription state, paid-through date, renewal setting, test flag and validated Play order reference. The sign-in account may differ from the Play purchasing account. The read-only owner console never queries Play or changes access when opened. Snapshots can be stale after renewal, refund or cancellation. Trial/account checks preserve the last purchase snapshot. No conversations, contacts, media, chat identity keys, Google subjects, display names or raw tokens enter this directory.

Identifying directory/snapshot records remain until verified deletion or the end of the licensing service. The existing verified Delete licensing account action removes that directory while retaining original trial eligibility and keyed purchase-ownership hashes for the service lifetime to prevent resets/reassignment. Minimal keyed deletion timestamps also remain to reject earlier in-flight writes. Earlier in-flight verification cannot recreate a deleted directory; a later fresh verification after disclosure may create it again. Support ID/email alone do not prove ownership. Existing anonymous records are not backfilled or guessed. Normal launch and nearby communication remain local-only for licensing. Collection is default-off until the updated legal/client rollout and explicit server enablement.
