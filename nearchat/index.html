A CONVERSATION THAT STAYS CLOSE.

Your people.
Your shared moments.

01 / CONNECT

Nearby conversations.

Direct messages use Bluetooth and Wi-Fi between approved phones. Both phones must keep NearChat open. Range varies; messages do not relay through strangers.

02 / SHARE

More than a message.

Share photos, files and recorded voice notes. Bring your circle together with groups, polls, events, statuses and search.

03 / TRUST

Know who is there.

Approve connections and compare codes. Verify safety numbers independently; a nickname alone does not prove identity. Chats and attachments use encrypted local storage.

Live voice calls and location sharing are unavailable in this version. Microphone access is used for voice notes only.

ONE PLAN FOR THE AVAILABLE APP FEATURES.

15 days to try.
Monthly or annual thereafter.

App use requires a subscription. Eligible new subscribers can receive a 15-day free trial on either plan when Google Play offers it.

Monthly

15 days free if eligible, then the full monthly price shown in Google Play. Automatically renews each month until cancelled.

Annual

15 days free if eligible, then the full annual price shown in Google Play. Automatically renews each year until cancelled.

Cancel in Google Play before the trial ends to avoid the first charge. Some accounts receive a paid offer without a trial. Review the actual localized price, eligibility and terms before confirming. Installation and onboarding do not start a trial.

Nearby messaging works offline while access is valid. Subscription activation and periodic checks require connectivity. A confirmed check permits up to 24 hours of offline use in the same device boot; restarting the app keeps that check, while rebooting the phone requires another Play check.

Billing, cancellation and offline access ↗

Your history stays yours.

There is no developer-operated messaging server, advertising or analytics service. Google Play services and Billing have their own data practices. Cancelling does not delete local history. Deleting local data or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.
