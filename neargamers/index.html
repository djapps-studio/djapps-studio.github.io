A local profile. A familiar opponent.

Choose a nickname and a built-in avatar. NearGamers keeps your profile, game history and local ratings on your phone, with no registration or cloud account.

Nearby play shares profile and match information with participating phones. Your display name and shortened device identity fingerprint are visible during discovery before connection approval. Optional match chat reaches other participants, and Google Nearby has its own diagnostics practices.

The Privacy Policy explains what stays on your phone, what other players receive, and how to delete local app data.
