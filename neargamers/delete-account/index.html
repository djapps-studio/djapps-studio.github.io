Delete your NearGamers licensing account

Updated 6 November 2026

NearGamers (com.neargamers.app) is published by Denny John, DJ Apps Studio. You can request deletion from the app or by email without reinstalling it.

Request deletion by email

Email an account deletion request

Send your request to support.djapps@gmail.com from your licensing Google account where possible. Include your NearGamers Support ID if available. If you no longer have the app or Support ID, identify the Google email used for NearGamers. Ownership verification may be needed before deletion. Never send passwords, verification codes, identity tokens or complete purchase tokens.

Request deletion in the app

Open the navigation menu, choose Demo & account, and use the licensing-account deletion control. Verify the same Google account and confirm the request. Active paid access is not required.

What is removed and retained

Deletion removes identifying support-directory details, including the verified email and directory purchase summary. Limited pseudonymous account eligibility, original demo dates and purchase-ownership records are retained to prevent demo resets and reassignment of purchases. These anti-abuse records have no automatic expiry. Account deletion does not start a new demo.

Local game profiles, avatar thumbnails and histories stay on the phone. Remove them through Android Settings > Apps > NearGamers > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Delete exported backup files separately. Other players may retain their copies of shared profiles, thumbnails and matches.

Subscriptions and other records

Account deletion does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel it separately in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > NearGamers. Google retains its own purchase and service records. Support correspondence has no fixed retention period; you can request its deletion in the same email, subject to applicable legal obligations.

See the NearGamers Privacy Policy for more detail.
